# T9: World transform and clipping

## Goal
Make `HandPaintEngine` follow the painter state: world transform and clip region are applied on the target.

## Behaviors
- B17: given a world transform (translate, scale) on the device painter, the result is that the rough shape lands at the transformed position on the target.
- B18: given a clip rectangle on the device painter, the result is that nothing is drawn on the target outside this clip.

## Tests to write
- Target: `QPainter` on a white 300×300 `QImage`. Device painter `translate(100,100)` then `scale(2,2)`, fill rect (0,0,20,20) black with no pen: pixel (120,120) is dark, pixel (20,20) is white, the dark pixels lie inside (100,100,40,40) enlarged by max_offset×2.
- Clip rect (0,0,50,50) on the device painter, fill rect (0,0,200,200) black: every pixel outside (0,0,50,50) stays white, pixel (25,25) is dark.
