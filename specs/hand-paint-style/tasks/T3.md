# T3: Vector arrows

## Goal
`drawPrimitive` of the 4 `PE_IndicatorArrow*` draws one filled triangle in `ButtonText` pointing in the asked direction, no pixmap.

## Behaviors
- B4: given `drawPrimitive` with `PE_IndicatorArrowUp`, `PE_IndicatorArrowDown`, `PE_IndicatorArrowLeft` or `PE_IndicatorArrowRight` on a rectangle R, the result is one filled triangle (a closed polygon of 3 points) inside R, in a solid brush of the palette `ButtonText` color, pointing in the asked direction (its tip is the point nearest the up, down, left or right side of R), and no image.

## Tests to write
- For each of Up, Down, Left, Right on R = (10,10,20,20): exactly one closed polygon of 3 points inside R, solid `ButtonText` brush, no image.
- Up: the triangle point with the smallest y is its tip (and alike for Down: largest y, Left: smallest x, Right: largest x).
