# T9: Scroll bar arrows

## Goal
Scroll bars draw their arrows with the vector arrows of T3 instead of Fusion's arrow pixmap.

## Behaviors
- B10: given a horizontal and a vertical `QScrollBar` with a range, rendered with the style, the result is no image and 2 arrows (B4) per scroll bar, in its 2 directions.

## Tests to write
- A horizontal `QScrollBar` (range 0..100, 180x16) rendered with the style (QWidget::render on RecordingPaintDevice, SketchTheme palette, HandPaintStyle): no image, one left and one right triangle.
- A vertical `QScrollBar` (range 0..100, 16x180) rendered with the style (QWidget::render on RecordingPaintDevice, SketchTheme palette, HandPaintStyle): no image, one up and one down triangle.
