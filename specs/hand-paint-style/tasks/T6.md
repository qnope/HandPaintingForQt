# T6: Disabled button colors

## Goal
The flat panel takes its colors from the palette group of the option state, so a disabled button uses the `Disabled` colors.

## Behaviors
- B14: given a disabled `QPushButton` rendered with the style, the result is that the fill and outline colors are the ones of the palette `Disabled` group.

## Tests to write
- A disabled `QPushButton` rendered with the style (QWidget::render on RecordingPaintDevice, SketchTheme palette, HandPaintStyle): its fill color is `palette.color(QPalette::Disabled, QPalette::Button)` and its outline color `palette.color(QPalette::Disabled, QPalette::Dark)` (use a test palette where Disabled and Active colors differ).
