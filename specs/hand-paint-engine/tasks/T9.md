# T9: Painter transforms

## Goal
The paint engine follows the painter transform (from `updateState`) so that shapes are drawn hand-drawn at their transformed place.

## Behaviors
- B16: given a painter with `translate(50,0)` (and separately `scale(2,2)`), when a rectangle is drawn, the result is a hand-drawn outline of the transformed rectangle.

## Tests to write

- `scale(2,2)` then `drawRect(20,20,40,40)`, NoBrush: hand-drawn outline of the rect (40,40,80,80).
