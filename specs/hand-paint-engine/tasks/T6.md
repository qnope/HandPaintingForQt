# T6: Rough curves (ellipses and paths)

## Goal
Make curved shapes hand-drawn: `drawPath` flattens the curves of a `QPainterPath` into segments and roughens them, so ellipses (which reach `drawPath`) and Bézier paths look hand-drawn.

## Behaviors
- B7: given a pen and `NoBrush`, when `drawEllipse(QRectF(40,40,120,120))` is called, the result is a hand-drawn outline of the ellipse.
- B10: given a pen and `NoBrush`, when `drawPath` is called with a `QPainterPath` made of a line and a cubic Bézier curve, the result is a hand-drawn outline of that path.

## Tests to write
- `drawEllipse(40,40,120,120)`, NoBrush: hand-drawn outline of the circle (center (100,100), radius 60); pixel (100,100) is background.
- Path `moveTo(20,150)`, `lineTo(80,150)`, `cubicTo((100,20),(140,20),(180,150))`, NoBrush: hand-drawn outline of that path.
